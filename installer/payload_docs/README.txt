STORY LAB
=========

Two characters, a world you describe, and a story that answers what you
type - told aloud, and (if you installed them) painted and scored with
background music.

  Play:            the "Story Lab" icon on the desktop or in the Start menu
  Add pictures or music,
  resume or repair: Start menu > Story Lab > Story Lab Setup

Picture styles (chosen on the setup screen, SCENE STYLE)
  Classical · safe      the place of each round, painted empty, plus a
                        portrait of each character (Nova Furry XL)
  Medium · manageable   both characters in the scene, drawn by Nova Furry
                        XL - a little unruly: now and then a third person
                        wanders in
  Cinematic · advanced  both characters drawn in the scene, in what the
                        story has them wearing now (Anima)
  Portraits follow the clothes your events give the characters.

Music
  A new instrumental track at the end of every round, made from its mood
  and the story's genre (ACE-Step 1.5), put through an equaliser, looping
  under the voices until the next one. Play / pause, volume and
  regenerate are on the MUSIC card beside the portraits.

Where things are
  This folder           Story Lab, its Python, your stories and games
                        (stories\, saves\, runSaves\)
  ComfyUI_windows_portable\   the picture and music engine, if Setup
                        installed it, with its models in ComfyUI\models\
  Ollama                its own program; the story model is kept where
                        Ollama keeps models (your user folder\.ollama)
  storylab_paths.json   written by Setup: how Story Lab finds all of it

Needs an NVIDIA graphics card with about 12 GB of video memory for the
story, the pictures and the music (they take turns on the card). The
story alone runs on less. On a laptop, use its performance mode and its
main charger: a power-saving mode makes everything several times slower.
